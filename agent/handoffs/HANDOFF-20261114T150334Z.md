Parsing the fixture: **381 allocations / 150,297 bytes**, identical across two
consecutive runs. Guardrails: 420 allocations / 160,000 bytes.

## Backend Decision

`ustr` and `stats_alloc` are not in the offline registry index, so TASK-097
selected the documented in-house fallback: `IStr` as a newtype over `Arc<str>`
(16 bytes native, 8 bytes wasm32), to be implemented in TASK-099. Serde remains
plain strings. Large unique payloads stay out of the intern table and become
`Box<str>`.
