**Date**: 2026-11-10T17:37:00Z

## Implementation Summary

- **IR paint** (`crates/rupa-ir/src/paint.rs`)
  - `Color { r, g, b, a }` straight-alpha `f32` in `0.0..=1.0`, with
    `from_rgba8`, `from_rgb8`, `to_rgba8`, straight/premultiplied conversion,
    and named helpers.
  - `Paint::Solid(Color)`; `PaintId`; arena-backed `PaintStore` with
    `add`, `add_solid`, `get`, `iter`, `len`, `is_empty`.
- **Compiler paint** (`crates/rupa-compiler/src/paint.rs`)
  - `PaintValidator::parse_color`, `lower_color`, `lower_fill`,
    `lower_fill_to_store`.
  - Exact case-sensitive table of the 10 canonical named colors.
  - Hex parsing requires `#` plus 6 or 8 hex digits (digits case-insensitive).
  - `T001` invalid hex (format help), `T002` unknown named color (valid-name
    list plus Levenshtein suggestion).
  - `fill none` lowers to `None` without allocating paint.
- **AST/parser**: `Fill.color` is `Option<Color>`; parser handles `fill none`.
- **Validation wiring**: invalid shape fill colors and invalid document
  background colors are now reported.
- **Decision** `docs/decisions/DECISION-004.md` records color/alpha
  conventions and the `R300`/`R301` → `T001`/`T002` mapping.
