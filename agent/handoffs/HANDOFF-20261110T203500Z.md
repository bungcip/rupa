**Status**: Completed and verified

## Implementation Summary

- `render_composition` now accepts an optional device-space clip mask and
  passes it to both fills and strokes.
- Each layer instance builds a mask by filling its local rectangle transformed
  by the composed local-to-parent transform, so rotated and scaled instances
  clip the transformed rectangle rather than an axis-aligned box.
- Nested instances multiply their mask alpha with the enclosing mask, so
  content is clipped to every ancestor layer.
- Root-level content has no layer mask and remains bounded only by the canvas.
- Clipping is always enabled; no `clip` property was added (specification
  §6.4).
- Masks are antialiased; opacity and z-index behavior are unchanged.
- Decision recorded in `docs/decisions/DECISION-022.md`.
