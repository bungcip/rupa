## Deliberate Exclusion

Specification fences are not executed wholesale: most are fragments, and the
`Star Field` example uses provisional syntax (`repeat`/`random(...)` directly in
shape positions) that the current parser does not accept. Those snippets remain
documentation proposals until the corresponding language/parser work is done.
