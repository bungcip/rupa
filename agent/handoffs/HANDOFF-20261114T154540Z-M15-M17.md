## Known Issue: cold-CLI render target missed

End-to-end release medians over 40 alternating runs against a TASK-097 build:

| Example | Baseline | Final | Delta |
|---------|---------:|------:|------:|
| `examples/minimal.rupa` | 4.78 ms | 11.27 ms | +135.8% |
| `examples/layers.rupa` | 8.98 ms | 15.36 ms | +70.9% |
| `examples/text.rupa` | 5.27 ms | 12.29 ms | +133.1% |

The PNGs are byte-identical and `--version` is unchanged. The fixed ~5.6 ms
cost is `ustr`'s first-use global cache initialization (`INITIAL_CAPACITY =
1 << 20` pointers plus 4 MiB of bump storage across 64 bins); RSS rises from
3.6 MB to 10.9 MB even for `minimal.rupa`. The renderer and lowering paths are
unchanged, and TASK-101's paint benchmark (both builds using `ustr`) showed no
regression.

Decision: keep the memory/wasm/output wins and document the miss. Long-lived
hosts pay the initialization once. Follow-up backlog item: evaluate a lean
interner (smaller initial cache, or `Arc<str>`/indexed fallback) to remove the
cold-start cost.
